3 For the programming teacher who showed up

Dear Jegu Sensei,
thank you.

Thank you for seeing more in me than a grade or a quiet day. You made programming feel possible, and noticed when I needed a little extra patience.

You taught me more than the code on the screen. You taught me how to keep going, ask questions, and trust my own voice.

You made learning programming feel like an invitation. I am grateful to have been your student.

With all my thanks,
Your student

A little something to play

Take a moment.

A small pause for the one person who gave me so much of their time.

Memories -Nakanoke no Itsutsugo

A quiet soundtrack

For the long days
and the little wins.

A moment to pause and feel appreciated.
